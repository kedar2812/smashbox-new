Smashbox Productions
Where Brands Come to Life Through Powerful Visual Storytelling

Founded in 2008 by Taher Husain, Smashbox Productions is a Pune-based commercial photography and film production company specializing in creating impactful visual content for brands, businesses, architects, designers, manufacturers, and entrepreneurs. With over 18 years of industry experience, we help businesses communicate their products, services, and stories through compelling photography and cinematic films.

Our approach goes beyond simply capturing images. Every project begins with understanding your brand, your audience, and your objectives. We combine creative direction, technical precision, lighting expertise, and storytelling to create visuals that build trust, strengthen brand identity, and drive business growth.
