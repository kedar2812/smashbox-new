The name

Where ideas collide.

Smashbox was named for one belief — a collision of assorted ideas in one place. Photographers, filmmakers, editors, designers, developers, strategists and marketers in one studio, arguing their way to something none of them would have reached alone.

Every brand has a story. Every space has a personality. Every product deserves attention. Every campaign deserves purpose. Which is why we don't set out to create content. We set out to create impact.

The process

Five stages, no templates.

A national campaign, an architectural shoot, a corporate identity, a website or an influencer programme — every project runs the same route. No shortcuts, and every solution built around the business objective.

01 — DISCOVER
  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with your objectives, your audience and your market. Nothing gets shot, designed or posted until we agree what the work has to achieve.

  2. 02

    Strategize

    Positioning, messaging, mood boards, shot lists, channel plan and budget — the whole route signed off before anything is made.

  3. 03

    Create

    Studio, location, screen or press. Photography, film, identity, design and code produced by one team held to one standard.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Retouching, colour grade, edit, build and handover — in every crop, format and file your channels need. Fast.

  5. 05

    Connect

    Then we put the work in front of people — creators, media, press and your own channels — and measure it against the goals set in stage one.

Our story

Founded in 2008 with one softbox.

Taher Husain started Smashbox in Pune as a commercial photography and filmmaking company, built on a vision of a creative ecosystem where photography, film, design, technology and marketing work together instead of in isolation. Eighteen years on, that ecosystem is the business: photographers, filmmakers, editors, designers, developers, strategists and marketers under one roof, so nothing is lost between the brief and the final file.

Different perspectives. Different expertise. One shared vision — the collision of ideas that gave the company its name.
