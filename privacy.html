Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This site belongs to Smashbox Productions, Pune. It is built to need as little from you as possible. Here is everything it collects, and why.

When you send us a message

If you fill in one of the forms, we keep what you typed — your email address and, if you give them, your name, phone number and message — so that we can reply. It is read only by the studio, is never sold or shared, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to smashboxindia@gmail.com.

Visit counts

We count visits so we know which work people look at and how they find us. We do this ourselves, without Google Analytics or any other tracking company, and without cookies. For each visit we record:

  • the page, and which links or buttons were pressed
  • the site that sent you here, if any, and any campaign tag on the link
  • the type of device and browser, and the country your device's time zone points to
  • how long the page was open and how far down it was scrolled

Your IP address is not stored. To tell one visitor from another within a single day, it is mixed with a random code that changes every day and only the result is kept, so a visit today cannot be linked to one tomorrow, or traced back to you. Counts older than about thirteen months are deleted.

Everything else

The site remembers whether you chose the light or dark look, on your own device. Fonts come from Google Fonts and some photographs from Pexels, which see the request for the file as any website would.

Questions go to smashboxindia@gmail.com.
