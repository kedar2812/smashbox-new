The process

Four stages, no surprises.

01 — DISCOVERY
  1. 01

    Discovery

    We sit down with your brief, your audience and your competitors, then agree what the images actually need to achieve.

  2. 02

    Direction

    Mood boards, shot lists, styling, props and a lighting plan — signed off before a single light goes up.

  3. 03

    Production

    Studio or on location, with a full crew, tethered capture and you reviewing frames live as we shoot.

  4. 04

    Post & delivery

    Retouching, colour grade and edit, delivered in every crop and format your channels need. Fast.

The studio

Founded in 2008 with one softbox.

Taher Husain started Smashbox Productions in Pune on a stubborn belief that most product photography was lazy. Eighteen years on, the team runs everything in-house — creative direction, styling, lighting, capture, retouching and edit — so nothing gets lost between the brief and the final file.

  • High-end lighting and production quality
  • Concepts tailored to each brand, never recycled
  • Fast turnaround with meticulous retouching
  • One team from concept to final delivery
